Brainstorm

Click a bubble to select it, then drag it to move it; its replies come along. Start typing or press Tab to reply, Enter to edit, ← and → to fold and unfold, Delete to remove. Hover over a bubble for a moment and type to branch from it. Drag empty space to pan. Ctrl + click empty space to start a new message. Scroll to pan; pinch or Ctrl + scroll to zoom.
